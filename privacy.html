Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lekre Bedi Mamadou ("we," "us," or "our"), operating NoLazy, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the NoLazy mobile application (the "Service"), based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire.

By using NoLazy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address (used to create and manage your account)
  • Onboarding responses — how you describe yourself, your biggest learning challenge, your available time per day, and your skill/path selection
  • Your selected skill, sub-skill, and learning path
  • Your chosen app language
  • Display name and profile photo (optional)

Information generated through your use of the Service:

  • Learning progress — current week, completed items, XP earned, streaks
  • Puzzle/goal image you set and revealed pieces
  • AI Mentor conversation history
  • Shorts videos you like or save
  • Subscription status and billing cadence
  • Device push notification token (if you grant permission)
  • Last seen timestamp and app activity

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic usage data to operate and improve the Service
  • Crash reports and error logs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Generate and personalize your 52-week learning path
  • Power the AI Mentor with context about your skill and challenges
  • Track and display your learning progress, XP, and streaks
  • Send push notifications about your learning reminders and updates (if you opt in)
  • Process your subscription and manage access to paid features
  • Improve the Service through aggregate, anonymized usage analysis
  • Respond to your support requests

3. AI and Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude AI): Your onboarding answers and AI Mentor conversations are processed by Anthropic's API to generate personalized content. Anthropic's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

YouTube: NoLazy displays video content sourced from YouTube's public API. We do not store or control YouTube video content. YouTube's privacy practices are governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Supabase: Your data is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database provider. Data is stored on servers that may be located outside Côte d'Ivoire.

Firebase (Google): We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications to your device. Firebase is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Apple / Google: Subscription payments are processed through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We do not store your payment card details.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where consent is the legal basis

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nolazy.service@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

NoLazy is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly. Please contact us at nolazy.service@gmail.com if you believe we may have collected information from a minor.

8. Push Notifications

If you grant permission, we may send you push notifications about your learning reminders, weekly briefings, and streak alerts. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your access to the Service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent changes were made.
